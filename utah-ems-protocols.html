About this reference

A fast, offline lookup for the 2025 Utah EMS Protocol Guidelines (September 2025).

Not a substitute for clinical judgment, your agency's medical direction, or OLMC. Verify every dose against the official PDF before administration.

How the content was made

The text was automatically extracted from the official protocol PDF. Adult and pediatric dosing are separated by the document's own column layout. Extraction is accurate for standard guidelines, but formatting of a few embedded tables and the Avalanche triage algorithm diagram (an image) may be approximate or missing — check the PDF for those. Each guideline shows its official PDF page number.

Provider level colors

  • EMR · EMT · AEMT · Paramedic — scope increases from green to red. Higher levels may also perform lower-level interventions.
